# Garantstelling — invitation letter signed at the gemeente

When inviting someone from abroad to the Netherlands, the host may have to sign
a **Bewijs van garantstelling en/of particuliere logiesverstrekking** (IND Form
1310) at the gemeente. It is a guarantee that the host covers the visitor's
costs, and the gemeente legalises the signature — the form is signed in front of
an official, never at home.

## The rule that applies everywhere

- The form is **pre-filled but unsigned**; the signature happens in front of the
  gemeente official. A form signed at home cannot be legalised retroactively.
- The ID produced must be one the gemeente accepts — a **driver's licence is not
  accepted**; use a Dutch passport, ID card or residence permit.
- A married/registered partner must normally attend and co-sign.
- An appointment is booked in advance through the gemeente's site.
- The fee is per document, and each visitor needs their own form — including
  children.
- The legalised form is what the visitor uses in the visa application, and it is
  valid only for a limited window.

## Worked example: gemeente Den Haag

- **Service name:** `Handtekening legaliseren` —
  https://www.denhaag.nl/nl/akten-en-verklaringen/handtekening-legaliseren
- **Bring:** valid ID (passport / ID card / residence permit; **no driver's
  licence**), the pre-filled but **unsigned** IND Form 1310, and the partner if
  married or in a registered partnership.
- **Appointment:** book in advance via denhaag.nl for "Handtekening legaliseren".
- **Cost:** €17.80 per document.
- **Validity:** must be used within **6 months** of signing; valid for **5 years**
  from the signing date, or until the visitor leaves Schengen, whichever comes
  first.
- **Process:** fill IND Form 1310 (available at ind.nl) → book the appointment →
  bring ID + unsigned form → sign in front of the official → pay → the visitor
  uses the stamped form for the visa application.

Fees, validity terms and ID rules are re-issued per gemeente and change over
time: re-read the gemeente page before quoting a figure, and if the gemeente is
not Den Haag, look up that gemeente's own page for the same service name.

## Form section 3.1 — Reden voor uitnodiging

Write the reason in Dutch. For a friend's visit:

> "Ik nodig mijn vriend/vriendin [naam] uit voor een bezoek aan Nederland. Wij
> zullen samen de bezienswaardigheden bezoeken. Het doel is toerisme en culturele
> ervaringen. Tijdens het verblijf logeert hij/zij bij mij."

For a family visit, replace "vriend/vriendin" with "familielid" and specify the
relationship. Keep the stated purpose consistent with the visa type the visitor
applies for.
