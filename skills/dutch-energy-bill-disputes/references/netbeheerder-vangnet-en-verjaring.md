# Vangnet invoices and prescription — working detail

Figures below are labelled by year; re-verify before quoting them.

## Why the netbeheerder invoices you at all

When an address consumes electricity/gas without a contract with a leverancier,
the netbeheerder advances the cost and recovers it afterwards ("energieverbruik
zonder contract"). Its own site explains the mechanism and offers bezwaar and a
betalingsregeling. So the claim is legitimate in principle: the money questions
are which years are still payable, at which tariffs, and against whose title.

## Prescription / vervaltermijn

- Consumer supply of electricity and gas counts as consumentenkoop → art. 7:5
  jo. 7:28 BW, 2-year prescription. Under art. 3:313 BW the term starts the day
  after immediate performance could be demanded, i.e. when that year's bill fell
  due — not when the late correction invoice arrived. Both the binding-advice
  practice of the Geschillencommissie Energie and the underlying Gerechtshof
  reasoning follow this.
- Netbeheerder claims for its own services carry an additional statutory 2-year
  period: E-wet art. 31 lid 10 and Gaswet art. 12b lid 3 sub e — the claim must
  be made "binnen twee jaar nadat de vordering opeisbaar is geworden", failing
  which it lapses. Case law applied it to grootverbruikers as well, and on the
  facts before it barred everything relating to the period more than two years
  before the first concrete claim.
- Interruption: a written aanmaning/mededeling in which the creditor
  unambiguously reserves the right to performance (art. 3:317 BW), or recognition
  of the claim by the debtor (art. 3:318 BW), restarts the clock. This is why a
  part-payment on a contested multi-year invoice is dangerous.
- The shorter term presupposes consumer status. Business use of the address
  falls back to the ordinary 5-year term for periodic payment obligations
  (art. 3:307 BW). Confirm with the accountant/lawyer before invoking either.
- Practical arithmetic: compute (first claim date − 2 years), map it onto the
  billed period, and report the contested share of the period rather than a euro
  amount until the specification is available.

## Rate and tariff checks

- btw on electricity/gas/warmte was temporarily 9% for consumption 1 Jul –
  31 Dec 2022 and 21% otherwise; the 9% line therefore identifies the 2022 part
  of a split invoice.
- Energiebelasting is charged per kWh/m³ in brackets plus the annual
  heffingskorting ("vermindering energiebelasting"): 2022 was €681,63 excl. btw /
  €824,77 incl. btw per year, so a correctly pro-rated ~€170 line in a "…2022"
  block means that block spans about one quarter.
- Netbeheerder tariffs (aansluit-/transportvergoeding, capaciteitstarief,
  meettarief) are ACM-capped and published per year per netbeheerder; the
  vangnet levy on the energy itself is not a competitive market price.

## The contract question, and disconnection

Under the Energiewet (in force 1 Jan 2026) the regional netbeheerder may no
longer act as supplier. It demands a leveringsovereenkomst and otherwise
proceeds to afsluiten, charging the customer for the work; in one decided case
the customer also owed the incassokosten and the proceedings. An unused gas
connection still has to be removed or closed.
